My Lights — Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 23, 2026

My Lights is a personal smart-lighting controller that connects an Amazon Alexa account to a small web application so that its buttons can trigger lighting routines through Alexa.

Information processed

To provide account linking and operate the integration, My Lights may process an Amazon account identifier and the authorization tokens issued by Amazon during account linking. It also processes the Alexa directives and event messages needed to discover the integration's devices and trigger the configured lighting routines.

How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate the Alexa connection, receive authorized Alexa requests, and send the lighting events needed to operate the service. My Lights does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Integration credentials and related connection data are stored by the service in Cloudflare Workers KV. The application is hosted using Cloudflare, and Alexa account linking and smart-home messaging are provided by Amazon. These providers process information as necessary to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

My Lights does not rent or sell personal information. Information is shared with Amazon and Cloudflare only as needed to provide account linking, hosting, and the requested smart-lighting functionality, or when required by law.

Retention and disconnecting

Connection information is retained while it is needed to operate the linked service. You can disconnect My Lights from the Alexa app's skill settings. Amazon's account-linking revocation process is used to notify the service when authorization is revoked; associated connection data can then be removed.

Security

The service uses authorization credentials and server-side secrets to protect requests between its components. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to what is needed to operate the integration.

Children

My Lights is not designed for children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the service changes. The date at the top indicates when it was last revised.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with your My Lights connection, use the developer contact/support information provided with the My Lights Alexa skill.
